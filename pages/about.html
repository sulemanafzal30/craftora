The idea behind Craftora

Keeping craft traditions
in the conversation.

Craftora is a concept marketplace connecting curious customers with the skills, stories and places behind Pakistani handmade work.

Our story

Good design makes room for the maker.

Handmade objects carry more than function. They can hold local knowledge, material intelligence, family practice and a visual language that has evolved over generations.

Craftora imagines a modern marketplace where those details are visible — where customers can explore the object and the context together.

What guides us

Our mission and vision

01

Mission

Make Pakistani craft traditions easier to discover and help independent makers present their work with clarity and dignity.

02

Vision

A thoughtful digital marketplace where heritage skills can meet contemporary homes, gifting and everyday use.

03

Principles

Respect the maker, describe products honestly, value material and process, and make the shopping experience accessible.

A country of many traditions

Many places. Many ways of making.

From Ajrak printing in Sindh and blue pottery in Multan to carved woodwork in Chiniot, embroidered textiles and leather crafts, Pakistan’s craft landscape is diverse and deeply regional.
